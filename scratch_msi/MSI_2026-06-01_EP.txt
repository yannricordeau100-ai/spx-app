D-Fend Acquisition at a Glance
June 1, 2026
Acquisition at a Glance
 ● We entered into a deﬁnitive agreement to acquire D-Fend Solutions (D-Fend), an industry
   leader in counter-drone technology, including detection and mitigation, for a purchase
   price of $1.5 billion

 ● Expands our mission critical safety and security solutions into fast evolving
   counter-drone space

 ● Builds on our deep and long-term relationships across state, local, federal and
   international public safety and enterprise customers

 ● Complements our broad engineering expertise in mission-critical RF technologies,
   spanning from Land Mobile Radio (LMR) to MANET networks

 ● Safer Skies Act of 2025, marks a signiﬁcant shift by authorizing trained and certiﬁed
   state and local law enforcement to not only detect and track unauthorized drones but
   also, where permitted, to safely mitigate them
D-Fend Overview
● D-Fend is a leading provider of counter - UAS (Unmanned Aircraft System) solutions, that
  enables organizations to detect, locate, identify, and, where permitted, mitigate
  unauthorized drones

● D-Fend’s non-kinetic mitigation utilizes advanced RF cyber-takeover technology that can
  override and redirect communication, safely bringing unauthorized drones to the ground,
  keeping people and communities safe

● The ﬁeld-proven technology is already deployed across more than 30 countries, serving
  airports, critical infrastructure, stadiums, borders, military bases, and enterprise
