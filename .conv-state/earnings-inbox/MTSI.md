FAIR VALUE AND FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS We group our financial assets and liabilities measured at fair value on a recurring basis in three levels, based on the markets in which the assets and liabilities are traded, and the reliability of the assumptions used to determine fair value. These levels are: Level 1 - Quoted prices in active markets for identical assets or liabilities. Level 3 - Fair value is derived from valuation techniques in which one or more significant inputs are unobservable, including assumptions and judgments made by us.
