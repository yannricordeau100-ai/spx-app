EBITDA and EBITDA margin UMG considers EBITDA and EBITDA margin, non-IFRS measures, to be relevant measures to assess its operating performance. It excludes restructuring expenses, which may impact period-to-period comparability. EBITDA margin is EBITDA divided by revenue. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin The difference between EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA consists of non-cash share-based compensation expense and certain one-time items that are deemed by management to be significant and incidental to normal business activity. Adjusted EBITDA margin is Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
