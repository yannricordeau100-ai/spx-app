Message from Our CEO $10.3B 25K+ 140+ 80+ $990M+ Business Model and Value Chain Alcon’s upstream activities focus on materials sourcing, supplier management and product design. We procure thousands of materials globally and work with a diverse supplier base to ensure product continuity. We prioritize product quality and safety and require compliance with evolving environmental and human rights standards. Functions such as Procurement, Business Continuity and Supply Chain Risk Management oversee dual sourcing, country-of-origin tracking and contingency planning for critical components. Before engaging suppliers, and periodically thereafter, Alcon conducts risk-based screening and due diligence, including labor rights and other risk assessments. All suppliers must commit to the Third Party Code of Conduct, which outlines our values and expectations related to labor rights, health and safety, the environment and other critical areas. Alcon manufactures at 17 sites across the globe. Operational activities encompass manufacturing, assembly and quality control across both the Surgical and Vision Care businesses. Highly automated production lines handle processes like compounding, chemical processing, molding and sterilization for contact lenses and eye care solutions, whereas precision manufacturing supports implantables, instruments and equipment. Through these downstream touchpoints, Alcon not only delivers high-quality solutions but also strengthens its relationships with customers, reinforcing its commitment to advancing eye health and sustainable innovation. World-Class Workforce At this stage, R&D and QA teams collaborate to evaluate material performance and regulatory requirements such as the European Union’s (EU’s) Medical Device Regulation and the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) regulation. Early product development and clinical research establish the foundation for innovation while embedding risk management and sustainability considerations into design. Throughout production, QA teams provide oversight through inspection, testing and certification under International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 13485:2016 and US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) standards. We strive to minimize our environmental footprint by focusing on efficiency and optimization to reduce emissions, water, waste and chemicals of concern. This commitment is integrated into Alcon’s operations through a global Health, Safety and Environment Management System. Resource planning teams also support efficiency and resilience. Continuous feedback between R&D, manufacturing and regulatory functions ensures that safety, quality and environmental standards are maintained at every stage. Downstream activities connect Alcon’s products with customers and capture insights that drive improvement. Finished products are packaged, sterilized where appropriate and distributed through global logistics hubs and wholesalers to eye care professionals and patients worldwide. Marketing, sales and training programs, including the Alcon Experience Centers and Alcon Experience Academy, help professionals stay current with the latest surgical and vision care innovations. The Lens, Alcon’s global policy on professional practices, outlines rules for responsible engagement with healthcare professionals and other key influencers in healthcare purchasing decisions. Customer service and feedback loops feed directly into R&D and QA to refine products and identify emerging needs.
