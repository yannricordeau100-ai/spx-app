Activities

The expertise of our employees is the basis on which our core construction competence relies, and it has been that way for 150 years. Owing to our long-term local presence and our extensive technical and project-management know-how, we are capable of realizing unique solutions and of driving on digitization in the construction industry.

Integrated Solutions

HOCHTIEF Group companies Turner and CIMIC offer comprehensive end-to-end solutions for large-scale building, civil engineering and industrial projects. Our services range from engineering, project management, supply chain and procurement services to the execution of civil works and industrial and infrastructure maintenance services.

Learn more

Engineering and Construction

Our core business is modern buildings, energy systems and transportation routes. However, we are also planning, developing and constructing more and more high-tech projects such as data centers.

Learn more

Infrastructure Investment

When state institutions and private companies work closely together, our infrastructure remains efficient. That’s why we primarily participate in major transportation projects through concessions.

Learn more
