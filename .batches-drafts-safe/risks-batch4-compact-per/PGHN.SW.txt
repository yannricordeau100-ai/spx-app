[4] Credit risk The following sections present the Group’s exposure to credit risk and how it is managed by the Group.
[5] Credit risk is the risk of financial loss to the Group if a customer or counterparty to financial instruments fails to meet its contractual obligations and typically arises from the Group’s trade and other receivables, loans, and cash and cash equivalents.
[6] The Group reassesses the credit risk for trade and other receivables on a regular basis by calculating the expected credit loss for such receivables.
[7] As of the reporting date, no material receivables were overdue (31 December 2019: none).
[8] The Group periodically also reviews its customer exposure and concentration.
[9] As of 31 December 2020, there is no substantial concentration of credit risk (31 December 2019: none).
[10] Other counterparties of the Group are typically regulated financial institutions or institutional investors with a high credit quality and, to a lesser extent, portfolio companies.
[11] Based on its assessment as of 31 December 2020, the Group has not identified any material expected credit losses (31 December 2019: none).
[12] The COVID-19 pandemic did not change the Group’s assessment with regard to the credit risk related to trade and other receivables. (b) Loans The Group’s loans (see note 5.3.4.) are typically granted to various investment programs managed by the Group on behalf of its clients.
[13] The Group reassesses the credit risk of its loans (see note 5.3.4. and note 5.3.5.) on a regular basis by calculating the expected credit loss for its loans.
[14] As of the reporting date, no material loans were overdue (31 December 2019: none).
