Unaudited Summary of Consolidated Net Revenues Our revenues are classified into the following two categories: • Transaction revenues : Net transaction fees charged to merchants and consumers on a transaction basis based on the Total Payment Volume (“TPV”) completed on our payments platform. Growth in TPV is directly impacted by the number of payment transactions that we enable on our payments platform.
