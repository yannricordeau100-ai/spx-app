All this paving the way for the next step in Tele2’s evolution and immediately following the merger with Com Hem we took the first step towards becoming an integrated operator through the launch of FMC offers in Sweden. In the Sweden Consumer segment, total EUSR was flat as growth in core services was offset by decline in legacy services and Landlord & other. Legacy services, including Mobile Prepaid, DTT and Fixed telephony & DSL, remain a drag on overall growth with a combined decline of 12 percent.
