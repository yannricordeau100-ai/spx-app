Roper Technologies, Inc. (NYSE ROP) reported financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2022. Our enterprise continues to exhibit strong momentum fueled by double-digit software recurring revenue growth and robust product demand. As a result, we are increasing our full year guidance. Also in the quarter, the quality of our portfolio and business mix improved as we completed the divestiture of our project-based TransCore business.
