 may be external

managers are responsible.
In 2024, to comply with the new European CSRD
regulation, the Group has set up a dual materiality
analysis, presented in section 4.1.1.4 "Double materiality
analysis and management of impacts, risks and
opportunities".

   analysis carried out by the Group as requested by

             This integrated report gives a summary overview of Legrand and its operations. It
