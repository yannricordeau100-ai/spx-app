The grant of stock units, valued at $200,000 calculated pursuant to Financial Accounting Standards Board Accounting Standards Codification Topic 718, Compensation—Stock Compensation (with the number of units rounded up to avoid fractional units), represents the equity portion of Valero’s non-employee director compensation program.
