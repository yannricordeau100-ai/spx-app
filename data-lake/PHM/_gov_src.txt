Table of Contents Table of Contents March 13, 2026 Dear Shareholders of PulteGroup: On behalf of the full Board of Directors, I reaffirm our commitment to providing rigorous oversight of PulteGroup s management team as they continue executing the Company s long-term strategy to create shareholder value. Executing against this strategy, PulteGroup continues to generate strong returns while thoughtfully allocating capital to its business, returning value to shareholders, and maintaining a strong balance sheet. Despite challenging and variable market conditions in 2025, we are pleased to report that our PulteGroup management team delivered another year of strong operating and financial results that are in alignment with its strategic initiatives: Closed over 29,500 homes and generated home sale revenues of $16.7 billion. Reported full-year gross and operating margins of 26.3% and 16.9%, respectively. Generated cash flow from operations of $1.9 billion. Realized a return on equity* for the year of 18%. And, ended the year with $2.0 billion of cash, after investing $5.2 billion in the business and returning $1.4 billion to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends. In light of the interest rate volatility experienced in 2025 and its impact on affordability and buyer demand we want to recognize the strong performance of the entire PulteGroup organization in navigating a highly dynamic market environment. I also want to express the Board's appreciation for the over 20 years of exemplary service that Board member Brian Anderson has provided to PulteGroup. Brian helped the Company navigate the economic downturn in 2008 and helped us shape our current strategy and also chaired our Audit Committee for most of his tenure. We congratulate Brian on his retirement from Board service and thank him for his many contributions. We are pleased to welcome Kristin Gannon to our Board. Kristin joined our Board in February and brings extensive real estate and capital markets experience. We also look forward to Ben Schall joining the Board, subject to his election by our shareholders at PulteGroup s 2026 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. Ben's background will further strengthen the Board's consumer and real estate expertise. We look forward to your participation in PulteGroup s 2026 Annual Meeting of Shareholders, which will be held on April 29, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. ET. Our Board of Directors has fixed the close of business on March 5, 2026, as the record date for determining those holders of our common stock entitled to notice of, and to vote at, the Annual Meeting of Shareholders and any adjournments or postponements thereof. Given the opportunity for expanded shareholder participation, PulteGroup s 2026 Annual Meeting will again be held virtually. You will be able to access the live audio webcast of the meeting by visiting www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/PHM2026. To participate in the meeting, you must have your 16-digit control number that is shown on your Notice of Internet Availability of Proxy Materials or on your proxy card if you elected to receive proxy materials by mail. Please note that in-person attendance at the 2026 Annual Meeting cannot be accommodated. The Notice of Annual Meeting of Shareholders and Proxy Statement, both of which accompany this letter, provide details regarding the business to be conducted at the meeting, including proposals for: the election of directors; the ratification of the appointment of Ernst Young LLP as our independent registered accounting firm for 2026; and an advisory vote to approve the compensation of our named executive officers. The Board of Directors recommends that you vote FOR the eleven director nominees named in this Proxy Statement, and FOR each of Proposals 2 and 3. We have established processes and policies for assessing, identifying and managing material risks posed by cybersecurity threats. Our processes and policies are based upon the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework with our processes focused on: (i) developing organizational understanding to manage cybersecurity risks, (ii) applying safeguards to protect our systems, (iii) detecting the occurrence of a cybersecurity incident, (iv) responding to a cybersecurity incident, and (v) recovering from a cybersecurity incident. Where appropriate, these processes and policies are integrated into our overall risk management systems and processes. For instance, all of our employees with network access are required to complete information security and privacy training on an annual basis. We are also frequently working to improve our information technology systems and provide employee awareness training around phishing, malware, and other cyber risks to enhance our levels of protection. These increases had a direct impact on consumer home mortgage interest rates, causing rates to more than double during the second half of 2022 and remain at higher rates throughout 2023 and 2024. The resulting increased cost of borrowing, combined with home price inflation in recent years and inflation elsewhere in the economy, resulted in persistent affordability challenges for consumers throughout 2024. These challenges and the fluctuations in interest rates during 2024 resulted in increased volatility in our new order pace in 2024. Most analysts believed that turbulent demand conditions would continue into 2025 as many economic indicators pointed to interest rates remaining elevated and economic conditions showing limited prospects for improvement in 2025. These persistent affordability challenges for consumers and other economic indicators served as the backdrop when the Company prepared its budget for 2025 and the performance targets to be used under the Company's incentive programs. Although the rate of inflation eased somewhat in 2025, and the Federal Reserve continued cutting their benchmark interest rate in 2025, mortgage interest rates remained elevated through the fourth quarter of 2025. Elevated interest rates. low consumer sentiment and other economic challenges contributed to weaker than expected consumer demand in 2025. We responded to these consumer sentiment and affordability challenges in 2025 by maintaining sufficient levels of spec inventory to service buyers seeking to close within 30 to 90 days; and rolling out sales price discounts and increased incentive strategies. We knew these pricing strategies would decrease gross margins from 2024 to 2025.
