Engineering and Construction
The Engineering and Construction segment encompasses HOCHTIEF’s activities in Europe, plus a 38.2% stake in FlatironDragados.
Our European activities comprise sustainable solutions for digital, social, energy, defense, and transportation infrastructure. The companies specialize in the entire asset and infrastructure project life cycle, including feasibility studies, design, planning, and investment, as well as construction, operation, and maintenance. The primary focus is on the German, Polish, Czech, Slovakian, Austrian, UK, Scandinavian, and Dutch markets.
FlatironDragados is the second-largest civil engineering company in North America and delivers essential infrastructure projects. Resulting from the merger of the two North American companies Flatiron and Dragados, the company is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.
