Abbott s diversified business model delivers leading shareholder returns Abbott s strong, sustainable performance has resulted in total shareholder return (TSR) significantly exceeding the peer group median on a one-year, three-year and five-year basis. In 2025, Abbott s one-year total shareholder return (TSR) was 13%, delivering shareholder returns exceeding the 7% peer group median. On a three-year and five-year basis, our TSR performance was 21% and 25%, respectively, performing above the peer group medians of 6% and 17%. Relative to our peers, on a one-year basis our TSR performance placed us at the 63rd percentile. On a three-year and five- year basis our TSR performance placed us at the 68th and 58th percentile, respectively.
