Mr. Hall noted that his decision to resign is not as a result of any disagreement with the Company, MEUS, their management, the Board or any committee of the Board. As a result of Mr. Hall’s resignation as a director, the Board is reducing the size of the Board from ten to nine directors, effective as of August 1, 2026 and pursuant to the Fourth Amended and Restated By-laws of the Company, as amended through November 6, 2024.
