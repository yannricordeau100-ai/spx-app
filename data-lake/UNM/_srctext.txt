CHATTANOOGA, Tenn. (April 28, 2026) - Unum Group (NYSE: UNM) today reported net income of $232.0 million ($1.41 per diluted common share) for the first quarter of 2026, compared to net income of $189.1 million ($1.06 per diluted common share) for the first quarter of 2025. Included in net income for the first quarter of 2026 is a net after-tax investment loss on the Company’s investment portfolio of $4.0 million ($0.03 per diluted common share) and the Closed Block segment after-tax adjusted operating loss of $116.5 million ($0.70 per diluted common share). Included in net income for the first quarter of 2025 is a net after-tax investment loss on the Company’s investment portfolio of $163.4 million ($0.91 per diluted common share) and the Closed Block segment after-tax adjusted operating income of $3.7 million ($0.02 per diluted common share). Excluding the items above, after-tax adjusted operating income was $352.5 million ($2.14 per diluted common share) in the first quarter of 2026, compared to $348.8 million ($1.95 per diluted common share) in the first quarter of 2025.
