(NASDAQ HAS), a global branded entertainment leader, today reported financial results for the third quarter 2022. These results were primarily impacted by the acceleration of Consumer Products shipments by our retailers into the second quarter due to anticipated supply chain challenges, as well as MAGIC THE GATHERING set releases and entertainment content scheduled for release in the fourth quarter versus the third quarter last year.
