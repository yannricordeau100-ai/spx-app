To my fellow stockholders: On behalf of our Board of Directors (the "Board"), I am pleased to invite you to attend NetApp, Inc. s Annual Meeting of Stockholders on Wednesday, September 10, 2025, at 3:30 p.m. Pacific time. This year s meeting will again be held virtually to provide a consistent and convenient experience for all, regardless of location, while providing substantially the same rights as an in-person meeting. Your vote is important, and we encourage you to vote promptly, even if you plan to attend the virtual meeting. Fiscal 2025 was a period of record performance for NetApp, Inc. ("NetApp"). We ended the year with some of the strongest financial results in our history, including all-time highs for revenue, gross profit, operating margin, and operating profit, reflecting both the effectiveness of our strategic focus and overall strength of our business model. We accelerated innovation that enabled significant market share gains in all-flash storage and substantial revenue growth in our cloud storage services. Against the backdrop of a dynamic macroenvironment, we delivered outstanding results across our portfolio by relentlessly prioritizing our growth opportunities in artificial intelligence ("AI") and enabling our customers to achieve increased efficiencies. Over the course of the year, we announced several key partnerships that dramatically expanded our AI ecosystem, including more collaborations with some of the largest hyperscaler cloud providers. As we look to the future, we remain committed to delivering sustainable, long-term value for our stockholders through innovation, operational excellence and the delivery of data-driven strategies for our customers. Positioning NetApp to achieve sustained top-line growth. Our record results in fiscal 2025 were underpinned by the strong growth of our all-flash systems and public cloud services. We refreshed our systems portfolio to offer customers faster, simpler, more scalable, and more affordable data infrastructure solutions. We also focused our public cloud services to emphasize our highly differentiated first party and marketplace cloud storage services and completed the sale of Spot by NetApp to Flexera Software LLC. Enabling AI transformation. The growing urgency among enterprises to modernize data infrastructure, accelerate cloud transformation and enhance cyber resiliency is driving significant demand for our AI solutions. In the last year alone, our enterprise AI business grew five-fold, and we closed on approximately 150 AI infrastructure and data lake modernization deals in the fourth quarter. Some of the biggest enterprises, as well as governments, are relying on our AI-ready intelligent data infrastructure, offered through the world's largest cloud providers, to enhance efficiency and data-driven decision making, and we are continuing to invest to capitalize on this opportunity and capture market share. Executing with a focus on operational excellence. At NetApp, operational excellence is a core part of our strategy, and we were pleased to achieve profitability records in fiscal 2025. As we continue to build on our strong foundation of actively managing the business for efficiency, we were pleased to welcome Wissam Jabre as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer in March 2025. Wissam brings deep experience leading finance organizations and a strong track record of disciplined operational management, and he is already having a positive impact. The entire team is focused on driving continued growth, increasing profitability and free cash flow, and delivering sustainable long-term value to our stockholders. Enriching the Board s expertise in an era of data and intelligence. We are committed to maintaining an independent, refreshed and dynamic Board of Directors who reflect a broad range of perspectives, tenure and expertise. In March 2025, we were pleased to welcome Frank Pelzer to our Board, increasing our number of directors to ten, nine of whom are independent and 50% of whom have been appointed within the last five years. Frank brings extensive financial and operational leadership experience within cloud and as-a-service companies, further strengthening our ability to grow and innovate in a data-driven future. Engaging with stockholders. The Company s year-round engagement with our stockholders is a critical part of our Board s decision-making process. The input the Board receives from conversations with investors on topics such as business strategy, corporate governance and board composition is invaluable, especially in a dynamic environment marked by rapid technological advancement and ongoing macroeconomic uncertainty. During fiscal 2025, key focus areas in our conversations with stockholders included Board skills and refreshment, executive pay, and sustainability. Looking ahead, we are entering fiscal 2026 with strong momentum. While the macroeconomy remains uncertain, we are confident in our ability to capture the opportunities ahead and create value for our stockholders. We also believe our executive compensation program provides a strong competitive advantage for NetApp and is an important to attract, retain and recognize our leadership and key talent. In designing an effective compensation program structure, the Talent and Compensation Committee follows the following principles: Pay for performance aligning pay with performance across leadership priorities to support stockholders interest in creating long-term and sustainable results; Appropriate pay levels ensuring pay levels are reasonable based on company performance and market context; and Strong governance structuring our program with a balanced incentive design to promote the successful execution of our strategic objectives while avoiding excessive risk taking. Our Fiscal 2025 Compensation Highlights and Target Pay Mix The pay mix for our chief executive officer ("CEO") and other Named Executive Officers ("NEOs") for fiscal 2025 is largely performance based with a significant percentage provided in at-risk pay. As described further in our Compensation Discussion Analysis ("CD A"), our financial operating performance in fiscal 2025 was strong, but fell short of our high expectations, resulting in below-target bonuses. Our fiscal 2023 2025 performance-based restricted stock units ("PBRSUs") were also earned below target based on three-year relative total stockholder return and Billings performance for the fiscal 2023 2025 performance period. The Board recommends voting for this proposal with our demonstrated pay alignment, continued focus on evolving our compensation programs in support of business objectives, commitment to stockholder engagement and history of strong Say-on-Pay results. The policy requires that the Board nominate for election or re-election as director only candidates who have tendered, in advance of such nomination, an irrevocable, conditional resignation that will be effective only upon both (i) the failure to receive the required vote at the next stockholders meeting at which they face reelection; and (ii) the Board s acceptance of such resignation.
