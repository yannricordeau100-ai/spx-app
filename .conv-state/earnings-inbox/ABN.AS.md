We earn money from the interest clients pay on mortgages and loans, and from fees and commissions for other services. Our loans to clients are funded by deposits and money borrowed from capital markets. We pay interest on these sources of funding. The difference between the interest we earn on loans and the interest we pay on deposits and capital market funding is our net interest income. We also earn money from fees and commissions through transaction banking, wealth management, advisory services, clearing and capital markets. We carefully manage the risks of providing loans and use the money we earn to pay for our costs, reinvest in the bank and deliver returns to our investors.
