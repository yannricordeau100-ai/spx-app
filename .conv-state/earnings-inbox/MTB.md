The results of operations acquired in the Hudson City transaction have been included in the Companys financial results since November 1, 2015. After application of the election, allocation and proration procedures contained in the merger agreement with Hudson City, M&amp;T paid $2.1 billion in cash and issued 25,953,950 shares of M&amp;T common stock in exchange for Hudson City shares outstanding at the time of the acquisition. The purchase price was approximately $5.2 billion based on the cash paid to Hudson City shareholders, the fair value of M&amp;T stock exchanged and the estimated fair value of Hudson City stock awards converted into M&amp;T stock awards. The acquisition of Hudson City expanded the Companys presence in New Jersey, Connecticut and New York, and management expects that the Company will benefit from greater geographic diversity and the advantages of scale associated with a larger company. The Hudson City transaction has been accounted for using the acquisition method of accounting and, accordingly, assets acquired, liabilities assumed and consideration exchanged were recorded at estimated fair value on the acquisition date.
