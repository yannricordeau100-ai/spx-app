Engineering and Construction
HOCHTIEF executes building construction activities in Germany and neighboring countries for public and private clients with specialized competence in certified, sustainable construction.
We are also delivering an increasing number of sustainable solutions in energy transition, digital, and social infrastructure. Furthermore, we are a leading player with our civil engineering activities in Europe (HOCHTIEF brand) and North America (FlatironDragados) where we differentiate through superior technical resources, skills and know-how delivering sustainable transport infrastructure solutions primarily via collaborative contract models.

Global Engineering Network
HOCHTIEF provides clients with the best technical expertise from our world-leading engineering and construction specialists wherever we are working. This is supported by the Group’s Global Engineering Network that is always able to respond to complex client requirements by offering innovative solutions. With this strategic network, we are able to quickly mobilize and assemble expert teams from across the Group, whether this is for advanced technology, energy transition, sustainable mobility, social infrastructure, or large-scale construction projects. Tailored to the client's vision, these project teams, supported by a unique knowledge infrastructure, turn state-of-the-art ideas into reality.
