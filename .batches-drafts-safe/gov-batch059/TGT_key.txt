Short-term incentives All NEOs are eligible to earn cash awards under our STIP program, which is designed to motivate and reward executives for performance on key annual measures. The financial component of our STIP program is based on two financial metrics: Net Sales (50%) and Incentive Operating Income (50%). See the “Performance metrics” tables and footnotes on page 42 for a description of how Net Sales are reported and how Incentive Operating Income is calculated from our financial statements. Beginning in Fiscal 2025, we replaced Merchandise Sales with Net Sales under the Financial component of STIP to better align incentives with overall revenue performance across all business streams.
