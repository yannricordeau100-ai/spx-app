2.2 Operational risks
  Major crises

Impact                          Probability



  Description of the risk
  Major crises comprise shocks capable of having a sudden, simultaneous impact on the Group’s operations: armed
  conflicts, geopolitical instability, trade tensions, political fragmentation and extreme events that require a crisis unit
  to be set up. Such situations can disrupt supply chains and key markets, increase costs (raw materials, energy and
  transportation) and generate a high level of regulatory and financial volatility. They can also lead to local business                  –––
                                                                                                                                          –––
  interruptions, compromise operational continuity and staff safety, and increase the risk of non-compliance with                         –––

  Cybersecurity and IT systems
Impact                         Probability



  Description of the risk
  The Group is exposed to risks related to the loss, disclosure or corruption of sensitive data, and to both targeted and
  large-scale cyberattacks that could compromise the integrity, availability or confidentiality of its digital assets. These
  threats may take the form of intrusions, large-scale malicious campaigns (phishing, dissemination of malware or
  ransomware, theft of login details), spying for the purposes of stealing strategic information, and fraud committed by
  internal or external persons exploiting technical or organizational vulnerabilities. They may cause major operational
  disruption, outages of critical services, the manipulation or corruption of data, financial losses and long-lasting
  damage to the Group’s reputation. The constantly shifting threat landscape, the growing complexity of technological
  environments and increasing dependency on information systems mean that Kering must enhance its prevention,                           –––
